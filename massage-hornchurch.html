Jazmine Sport & Thai Massage is a massage shop in Elm Park, Hornchurch. We have been on Tadworth Parade for over ten years, offering traditional Thai massage alongside sports, deep tissue and relaxing oil treatments, seven days a week.

Where we are

You will find us at 14 Tadworth Parade, Hornchurch RM12 5AS. Elm Park station on the District line is a three-minute walk, so we are easy to reach from across Hornchurch, Romford, Upminster and Dagenham. There are council parking spaces nearby, free after 6:30pm Monday to Saturday and all day on Sunday.

Massage treatments in Hornchurch

Thai massage: traditional stretching and pressure work, often done clothed. Our most popular treatment.

Sports massage: for runners, footballers and gym-goers, before or after training.

Deep tissue massage: slow, firm work for stubborn tight areas.

Swedish and aromatherapy massage: gentle oil massage to relax and unwind.

Neck and shoulder and foot massage: short, focused treatments for one area.

We also offer pregnancy massage, cupping therapy and four hands massage. Prices start at £35 for 30 minutes. See all treatments and prices.
