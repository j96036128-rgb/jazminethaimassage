Which massage is right for you?

Thai massage is our speciality: traditional stretching and pressure work, often done clothed. It suits stiff, tired bodies and anyone who sits at a desk all day.

Sports massage is for runners, footballers and gym-goers, before or after training or when you are carrying a niggle. Deep tissue massage is slow, firm work for stubborn tight areas such as the back, neck and shoulders.

To simply relax, choose Swedish or aromatherapy massage. For one problem area, a 30-minute neck and shoulder or foot massage is a good start. We also offer pregnancy massage, cupping therapy and four hands massage.
