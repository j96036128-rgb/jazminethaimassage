Thai massage

Thai massage in Hornchurch and Elm Park

Stretching and pressure work, the way it is taught in Thailand.

Traditional Thai massage combines firm pressure along the muscles with gentle, assisted stretches. The therapist uses hands, thumbs, forearms and sometimes knees and feet, moving you through a sequence that works the whole body. It is more active than an oil massage, and many people come away feeling both looser and more awake.

Who it suits

People who sit at a desk all day, anyone who feels stiff through the back, hips or shoulders, and those who like a firmer, more physical treatment. It can be made gentler, so say if it is your first time.

What to expect

Thai yoga massage is usually done in loose, comfortable clothing, without oil. A full body Thai massage works from the feet upwards and finishes at the head and shoulders. Four hands massage is the same idea with two therapists working together. Tell your therapist about any injuries or health conditions before you start, and speak up at any point if the pressure is too much.

When to choose something else

If you are pregnant, ask about our pregnancy massage instead. If you have a recent injury, see a physiotherapist or your GP first.
