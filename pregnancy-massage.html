Pregnancy massage

Pregnancy massage in Hornchurch and Elm Park

A gentle massage adapted for mums-to-be.

Pregnancy massage is a gentle treatment adapted for expectant mothers. The pressure is lighter than our other massages, and you are positioned comfortably with cushions for support.

Before you book

Please tell us how many weeks pregnant you are when you book, and mention anything your midwife or doctor has advised. If you have any complications, or are not sure whether massage is suitable for you, check with your midwife or GP first.

What to expect

The massage concentrates on the areas that tend to feel the strain, commonly the back, shoulders, legs and feet. Tell your therapist straight away if any position is uncomfortable or you would like a break. You can ask for unscented oil.
