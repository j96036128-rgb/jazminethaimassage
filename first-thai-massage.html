What to expect at your first Thai massage

Never had a Thai massage before? Here is what happens, what to wear and how to choose your first treatment.

If you have never had a Thai massage, the first thing to know is that it is not quite like the massage you may be picturing. There is more movement in it. Alongside pressure from the therapist’s hands, thumbs and forearms, a traditional Thai massage includes gentle stretches, where the therapist moves your arms, legs and back through positions you would struggle to reach on your own. People often describe it as being stretched and pressed at the same time.

Before you arrive

Wear or bring something loose and comfortable if you are having a traditional Thai massage, as it is often done clothed. For an oil massage you will be covered with towels throughout. Try not to eat a heavy meal in the hour beforehand, and arrive a few minutes early so you are not rushing.

When you get here

Tell your therapist about any injuries, aches or health conditions, if you are pregnant, and how firm you like the pressure. Please be open about this. It helps us make sure the treatment is right for you, and there are times when we will suggest a gentler treatment or ask you to check with your GP first.

During the massage

Thai massage can be firm, but it should never be painful. A useful rule is that pressure should feel like a “good ache”, the kind you can breathe through. If anything feels sharp, or simply too much, say so. Your therapist would much rather adjust than have you grit your teeth for an hour.

Afterwards

Most people feel looser and a little sleepy. Drink some water and take it easy for the rest of the day if you can. It is normal to feel slightly tender the next day after a firm treatment, much as you might after exercise.

Which treatment should I book?

If you are new and not sure, a 60-minute Thai or Swedish massage is a good place to start. If you train regularly or have a specific tight area, ask about sports or deep tissue massage instead. Tell us what you are looking for when you book and we will suggest the best fit.
