Aromatherapy massage

Aromatherapy massage in Hornchurch and Elm Park

A gentle, flowing oil massage with scented essential oils.

Aromatherapy massage is a relaxing oil massage in which essential oils are blended into the massage oil. The strokes are slower and lighter than a deep tissue or sports massage, and the scent is part of the experience. It is the treatment to choose when the aim is to switch off.

Who it suits

Anyone who wants to unwind more than they want firm pressure: after a demanding week, before or after travelling, or as a treat. It is a popular choice for gift vouchers.

What to expect

You will be covered with towels throughout and the therapist uncovers only the area being worked on. Pressure is light to medium and can be adjusted. Tell your therapist about any injuries, allergies or health conditions before you start. If you are sensitive to fragrances, or pregnant, say so when you book so we can suggest a suitable oil or a different treatment.

Add hot stones

Warm stones can be added to any oil massage. Many people find the heat helps them relax into the treatment.
