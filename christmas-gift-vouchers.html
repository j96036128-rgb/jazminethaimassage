Which treatment to choose

If you are not sure what they would like, an amount is the safest choice, and they can pick on the day. If you want to choose for them, here is a simple guide.

For someone who needs to switch off: a Swedish massage or aromatherapy massage. Gentle, flowing and very relaxing.

For someone with a stiff back or desk shoulders: a Thai massage, deep tissue massage or a focused neck and shoulder massage.

For a runner, footballer or gym-goer: a sports massage.

For a mum-to-be: a pregnancy massage, a gentle massage adapted for pregnancy.

For something a little special: a four hands massage, with two therapists working together.
