Cupping therapy

Cupping therapy in Hornchurch and Elm Park

Suction cups on the back and shoulders, often combined with massage.

In cupping, cups are placed on the skin, usually on the back and shoulders, and suction is created inside them. The cups are left in place for a few minutes or moved gently across the muscles. Many people have it alongside a massage.

About the marks

Cupping commonly leaves round marks where the cups have been. They can look dramatic but are not usually painful, and they generally fade within a few days to a couple of weeks. If you have an event coming up where your back will be on show, it is worth planning around that.

What to expect

You will feel a tight, pulling sensation under each cup. It should be strong but comfortable, so tell your therapist if any cup feels too much and it can be released at once. Tell your therapist about any injuries, allergies or health conditions before you start.

Who should avoid it

Cupping is not suitable for everyone. Please speak to us first, and check with your GP if in doubt, if you are pregnant, take blood-thinning medication, bruise easily, or have a skin condition, broken skin or sunburn in the area.
